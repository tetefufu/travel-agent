Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7. Las Vegas in June = very hot (38–42°C) but the Strip is entirely air-conditioned. Pool culture is at its peak — every hotel has epic pool parties. Perfect for pool lovers willing to embrace the heat. Book well in advance — June is busy. Recommended if you're pool-focused; consider Oct for milder weather.
