Eid al-Adha 2026: Jun 6–7. Absolutely perfect timing. June is Namibia's finest month — cool crisp days (22–28°C), cold star-filled nights, zero humidity, and the red dunes of Sossusvlei glow most intensely in the winter morning sun. Etosha game viewing peaks in June as animals concentrate at waterholes. Book lodges 6+ months ahead — winter is peak and fills fast. Highest recommendation for this Eid window.

🍽️ Restaurants & Vegetarian Guide

⚠️ Vegetarian note: Namibia is a meat-heavy culture (game meat — oryx, kudu, springbok are national staples). However, all luxury lodges will prepare bespoke vegetarian menus with advance notice — their chefs are world-class. In Windhoek, a small but growing selection of cafes cater to vegetarians. Always inform your lodge at booking. The good news: fresh vegetables, local cheeses, and international-standard lodge cooking means you will eat well.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
None — UK passport · 90 days visa-free
💵 Currency
Namibian Dollar (NAD) · 1:1 with South African Rand · 1 NAD ≈ 0.2 AED · Cards at lodges only; carry cash for small stops
💸 Tipping
Very important at lodges: N$100–200/day/person (~20–40 AED) · ranger tip: N$150–200/day
🚗 Getting around
Private charter flights between destinations strongly recommended (~1,100–2,200 AED/segment). Driving is possible but distances are extreme — Windhoek→Sossusvlei = 5.5h on gravel
🌡️ June weather
Sossusvlei: 22–28°C days, 5–10°C nights (cold!) · bring warm layers for pre-dawn dune walks
🦟 Health
Malaria risk: LOW in Sossusvlei and southern Namibia. HIGHER in Caprivi/northern areas. Consult travel doctor.
📷 Photography tips
Bring wide-angle (16–35mm) for dunes + long (300mm+) for wildlife. 16GB+ cards. Dead Vlei is best 6–8am only.
🔌 Connectivity
Very limited at lodges — expect little/no signal. This is intentional — embrace the disconnect.
