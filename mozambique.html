Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7. Perfect alignment. June = dry season beginning, 25–27°C, calm turquoise seas, humpback whale season starting. Strongly recommend June for Bazaruto. Book fly-in island lodges 6+ months ahead — extremely limited rooms.

🍽️ Restaurants & Dining

⚠️ Important Vegetarian Warning: Mozambique cuisine is heavily seafood and meat dominant. Prawns, crayfish (langostino), peri-peri chicken are the national dishes. Fine dining at island resorts is primarily seafood-focused. However: luxury lodges can prepare vegetarian meals with advance notice — always contact the lodge directly when booking. Expect pasta, grilled vegetables, rice dishes and local greens (matapa — cassava leaves with coconut). The Indian/Arabic influence in northern Mozambique means some vegetarian options exist in Pemba, but Bazaruto lodges will be the main dining.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
Visa on arrival · UK passport · ~$50–100 (≈184–367 AED) · 30 days · bring USD cash
💵 Currency
Mozambican Metical (MZN) · 1 USD ≈ 63 MZN · USD widely accepted at lodges
💸 Tipping
Lodge staff: $10–15/couple/day · guides: $10–20/activity · USD preferred
🚁 Getting around
Charter flights to islands organised by lodges. No DIY — all transfers pre-arranged.
💉 Health
Malaria prophylaxis required — consult GP. Yellow fever cert if coming from endemic country. Avoid tap water.
🗣️ Language
Portuguese (official) · Swahili in north · English at all lodges
🌡️ June weather
25–27°C · very low humidity · calm Indian Ocean · excellent visibility for diving
⚡ Electricity
South African plugs (Type M) · 220V · bring adapter
