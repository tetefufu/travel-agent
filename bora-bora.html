ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
UK passport — Visa-free · French Polynesia (France) · 90 days
🛂 USA Entry
ESTA (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) required for UK passport holders · apply online ($14 USD)
💶 Currency (FP)
CFP Franc (XPF) in French Polynesia · 1 USD ≈ 110 XPF · cards accepted everywhere
💵 Currency (CA)
USD in California · no conversion needed
💸 Tipping
French Polynesia: not customary, service included · California: 18–20% at restaurants
🚤 Getting around FP
Resort speedboats · single-island stay, no inter-island flights needed
🚗 Getting around CA
Rent a car or use ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft). California roads excellent.
🗣️ Language
French & Tahitian in Polynesia · English at luxury resorts · English throughout California
⏰ Time zones
FP: UTC-10 · California: UTC-7 · Dubai: UTC+4 · allow 1 day for adjustment
🌊 Water safety
Lagoon warm ~28°C · excellent visibility in May · reef-safe sunscreen mandatory
