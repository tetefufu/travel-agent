Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7 — Ideal timing. June is Bora Bora's dry season — low humidity, clear turquoise lagoon, perfect snorkelling visibility. The overwater bungalows are in peak condition. This is the best possible time to visit Bora Bora. Book hotels 6+ months ahead — June sells out fast. Eid al-Fitr (Mar 30–31) falls in the wet season — not recommended.
