Ultra Music Festival recommendation: If going in March, align your trip with Ultra Music Festival (Mar 27–29, 2026) — Miami's biggest electronic music event. VIP tickets are essential — general admission is severely overcrowded. Book hotels 4–6 months ahead; South Beach prices spike during Ultra weekend. For Eid al-Adha (Jun 6–7), Miami in June = hot and humid (30–33°C) with brief afternoon thunderstorms. Pools and beach life are incredible, hotel prices drop vs peak season. Plan beach/pool mornings, indoor/dining afternoons — excellent pool-luxury experience at better rates.
