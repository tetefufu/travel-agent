ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
Visa-free for UK passport holders · 30 days · Extendable at immigration office
💰 Currency
Thai Baht (THB) · 1 THB ≈ 0.11 AED · Exchange at Superrich or airports · ATMs ubiquitous
💸 Tipping
~50–100 THB (5–11 AED) in restaurants · 200+ THB at luxury hotels · Massage therapists: 50–100 THB
🚗 Getting around
Bangkok: BTS Skytrain + Grab (SE Asian Uber) · Phuket: hire driver or scooter (careful on roads)
🗣️ Language
Thai · English spoken in luxury hotels and tourist areas · Download Google Translate with Thai
⏰ Time zone
ICT: UTC+7 · 3h ahead of UAE
🌡️ Weather (Nov–Feb)
Bangkok: 26–32°C, low humidity · Phuket: 28–33°C, low rainfall · Perfect
🏥 Health
Drink bottled water only · Mosquito repellent (DEET) essential · Excellent private hospitals in Bangkok
