🍽️ Restaurants — Vegetarian Mexican Highlights

Mexico is one of the world's great vegetarian cuisines. Traditional Mexican food relies heavily on corn, beans, chillies, squash, avocado, and cheese — much is naturally vegetarian. Tell staff "soy vegetariano/a" and specify "sin carne, sin pollo, sin mariscos" (no meat, no chicken, no seafood).

Must-try vegetarian Mexican dishes:
• Tacos de rajas — roasted poblano strips + cheese on corn tortillas
• Enchiladas verdes / rojas — corn tortillas in green/red chilli sauce + cheese
• Chiles en nogada (seasonal Sep–Oct) — stuffed poblano in walnut cream + pomegranate
• Tlayuda Oaxaqueña — large crispy corn tortilla, black beans, quesillo cheese
• Elote callejero — street corn with mayo, cheese, lime, chilli
• Guacamole fresco — made tableside at good restaurants
• Huevos rancheros — fried eggs on tortillas with tomato-chilli salsa
• Sopa de lima (request no chicken) — lime-broth soup
• Churros con chocolate · Horchata · Agua fresca de Jamaica

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
Visa-free for UK passport holders · 180 days · No pre-approval needed
💰 Currency
Mexican Peso (MXN) · 1 MXN ≈ 0.2 AED · USD widely accepted in Tulum resort zone
💸 Tipping
10–15% at restaurants · expected at luxury resorts · 20% at Pujol/Quintonil level
🚗 Getting around
CDMX: Uber (safe and cheap) · Metro for budget days · Tulum: hire driver — roads unlit at night
🗣️ Language
Spanish · English spoken at luxury hotels, Tulum resort strip · Download Google Translate
⏰ Time zone
CDMX: CST UTC-6 · Cancún: EST UTC-5 · 9–10h behind UAE
🌡️ Weather (Nov–Mar)
CDMX: 16–22°C, cool evenings (bring jacket) · Tulum: 24–30°C, dry · Ideal combination
⚠️ Safety
CDMX: Polanco/Condesa/Roma safe for tourists · Tulum: safe resort strip — hire transport, avoid driving at night · Cab from hotel, not street
