Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7. Strong pick. June = warm Istanbul days (26°C), Bosphorus swimmable, Cappadocia balloons flying daily. Note: Eid Kurban Bayramı is a major Turkish public holiday — some local restaurants closed Jun 6–7, but tourist attractions stay open. Hotels and tour operators unaffected.

🍽️ Restaurants

🌿 Turkey is one of the world's top vegetarian destinations. Meze culture means dozens of plant-based small plates are standard. Mezes (hummus, ezme, sigara böreği with cheese, stuffed grape leaves, baba ganoush) are meat-free by default. Highly recommended for vegetarians.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
e-Visa required · UK passport · ~$50 (≈184 AED) · apply at evisa.gov.tr
💵 Currency
Turkish Lira (TRY) · 1 USD ≈ 32–35 TRY · 1 AED ≈ 8–9 TRY
💸 Tipping
10% in restaurants · porters 20–50 TRY · taxis: round up
🚗 Getting around
Istanbul: taxi or metro · Cappadocia: private driver or tour (~550–730 AED/day)
✈️ Internal flight
IST → Kayseri (ASR) or Nevşehir (NAV) ~1.5h · ~1,100–1,650 AED return pp
🗣️ Language
Turkish · English widely spoken at tourist sites and luxury hotels
🌡️ June weather
Istanbul 24–28°C · Cappadocia 22–28°C (cooler at night ~12°C)
🕌 Culture
Dress modestly at mosques (cover head/shoulders). Turkey is secular and very welcoming.
