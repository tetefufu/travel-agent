Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7 — Workable but plan carefully. June at Disney World is hot (32–34°C) with rising summer crowds. Use Early Entry (resort guests get in 30 min early), ride major attractions by 10am, then pool at Grand Floridian from 1–4pm, return for evening fireworks. Alternatively, consider November — EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival, lower crowds, perfect weather, significantly cheaper. Eid al-Fitr (Mar 30–31) window falls in a very good period.
