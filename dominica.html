Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7. Exceptional timing for Dominica. June = lush emerald rainforest, waterfalls at full flow, very few tourists, lowest hotel rates. Secret Bay's private villas will have full availability. Hot springs, boiling lake hikes and diving are all superb. The most exclusive, off-the-beaten-path option in this collection — for the adventurous luxury traveller.

🍽️ Restaurants

Dominica's cuisine is incredibly vegetarian-friendly — root vegetables, dasheen (taro), plantain, callaloo, breadfruit and tropical fruits feature heavily in all local cooking. Farm-to-table is the norm here, not a trend.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
Visa-free · UK passport · no prior visa needed
💵 Currency
Eastern Caribbean Dollar (XCD) · 1 XCD ≈ 1.36 AED · USD widely accepted
💸 Tipping
10–15% appreciated · Secret Bay included in some packages
✈️ Getting there
Via Antigua (ANU) or Barbados (BGI) — short hop to DOM (~30–45min)
🚗 Getting around
Hire car essential — island roads are winding mountain tracks. Driver hire recommended.
🌡️ June weather
27–30°C · Wetter than dry season but short showers. Rainforest is spectacularly green.
🗣️ Language
English (official) · Dominican Creole also spoken
🏠 Best base
Secret Bay (NW Dominica, 4 nts) + Rosalie Bay (East coast, 2 nts) — best of island contrast
⚠️ Important note
This is NOT Dominica Republic — it is the Commonwealth of Dominica, a separate Eastern Caribbean island
🐋 Unique wildlife
Resident sperm whales year-round · endemic purple-throated carib hummingbird
