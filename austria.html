🍽️ Restaurants & Vegetarian Guide

Austria is excellent for vegetarians. Vienna has one of Europe's best vegetarian fine-dining scenes, led by Michelin-starred Tian. Traditional Viennese cuisine has many naturally vegetarian dishes: Käsespätzle (cheese noodles), Eierschwammerl (chanterelles), and Topfenstrudel. Vienna's large Turkish/Middle Eastern community ensures halal & vegetarian options citywide. The famous Viennese Kaffeehäuser (coffee houses) all serve vegetarian pastries.
