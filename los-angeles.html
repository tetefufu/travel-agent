Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7 — Very good for LA. June in LA means warm afternoons (25–27°C), long days, and manageable crowds. The famous "June Gloom" (coastal morning fog) burns off by 11am — go to the pool after noon and you get perfect conditions. Beverly Hills hotel pools peak June–September. Eid al-Fitr (Mar 30–31) also excellent — Oscars buzz fading, wildflower season, lower hotel prices.
