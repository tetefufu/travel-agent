## Tips

### Planning
- Everything on the south coast is 20–40 min apart — plan by zone, don't backtrack daily
- Choose your base by vibe — your whole experience depends on it
- Don't change hotels daily
- Plan sunset spots in advance — evening traffic on south coast is real
- Ignore evening traffic at your peril: avoid booking party nights if staying in calm areas

### District Clarity (Very Important)
- **Galle District:** Galle · Unawatuna · Ahangama
- **Matara District:** Weligama · Mirissa · Madiha · Dikwella · Hiriketiya

### Trains
- **Kandy → Ella:** packed, booked weeks ahead — book via 12Go Asia or government railway website
- **Better option:** Ella → Badulla (~€0.50, ~1 hour, same landscapes, almost empty carriages)
- Sit on the **right side** for the best views
- Colombo Fort → Galle by train: one of the most scenic rides (ocean views)

### Nine Arch Bridge, Ella
- Skip the tuk-tuk — some drivers ask crazy prices
- **Walk ~30 min** or rent a scooter
- **Best time: 6:15–6:45am** — train passing + zero crowds = best photos
- Walk past the bridge along the tracks (15–20 min): tea fields, silence, even better shots

### Ella Waterfalls
- **Skip Ravana Falls** — overcrowded, busloads of people, slippery rocks
- **Do Upper Diyaluma Falls instead** (~45 min from Ella, go before 9am, free)
  - Natural infinity pools at the top, unreal views, far fewer people

### Little Adam's Peak
- **Don't start from Ella town side** — traffic jam of people
- Start from the **98 Acres side** instead
- Start ~4:30–5am, ~1 hour hike, sunrise ~6am, free (ignore tuk-tuk offers)

### Tea Factory
- **Halpewatte Tea Factory** — best reputable one near Ella
- 3,000 LKR (~€8–9) for guided visit, educational, well explained

### Ahangama Sunset Cocktails
- **@cactus_ahangama** — order the watermelon margarita
- **@hoteldeuncles** — order the banana negroni
- **@kai_ahangama** — coffee + incredible sea views

### South Coast by Vibe
| Area | Best for | Crowd |
|------|----------|-------|
| Mirissa | Party, nightlife, beach bars, whale watching base | Young, social, budget–mid |
| Weligama | Beginner surf, café hopping, relaxed | Digital nomad, chill |
| Hiriketiya | Small aesthetic bay, stylish cafés, good surf | Boutique, curated |
| Ahangama | Café hopping, photo spots, surf breaks, sunset drinks | Calm but cool |
| Unawatuna | Calm beaches, close to Galle, family-friendly | Families, relaxed |
| Galle Fort | Colonial history, sunset walks, boutique shopping | Half-day visit |

### Turtle Beaches
- Best near Unawatuna side — Dalawella Beach
- Private entry from Wijaya Beach restaurant
- More scenic, less party, perfect for slow beach days

### Mirissa Specifics
- **Secret Beach** — hard to get to but 100% worth it
- Parrot Rock + Coconut Hill — scramble for views
- "Lava" lantern beach café — viral spot, explore in the evening
- Whale watching: great base for blue whale tours (Nov–Apr season)

### Ahu Bay, Ahungalla
- Perfect first-night stop — not far from airport
- Best spot to decompress and recover from jet lag before exploring
- Hotel: **Ahangama Abode by the beach**

### Silent Beach
- Most beautiful beach in Sri Lanka (per multiple sources)
- Untouched paradise — total peace, relaxation, wellness
- Near Dikwella (Matara District)

### Yala National Park
- Sri Lanka's most well-known park — gets busy
- Book with the right operator — worth it
- Recommend staying within the park for up-close wildlife encounters

### Sigiriya
- Polonnaruwa Rock: THE best external viewpoint of Sigiriya Lion Rock (2–3 km away)
- Sigiriya Wildlife Safari: Sigiriya/Trincomalee/Kandulla area — see wild elephants (sometimes 100+)
- Book Lion Rock tickets in advance (UNESCO ~$30/person)

### Colombo
- Airport (CMB/Katunayake) is 35–40 miles from city — allow transfer time
- Tuk-tuk rides are a must-do experience through the city
- Street food crawl along Galle Face Green — classic evening activity
- Pettah Market: cheapest finds (clothes, electronics, chaotic fun)

### General
- Tourist vehicles have unlimited fuel — no fuel crisis concerns
- Train from Ambewela to Ella possible (book via 12Go Asia)
- Monsoon: May–Oct, down south gets rain — east coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay) stays dry

## Places to Visit

### Colombo
- **Gangaramaya Temple** — one of the most revered Buddhist temples, don't miss the museum
- **Seema Malaka** — floating temple on Beira Lake
- **Jami Ul-Alfar Mosque (Red Mosque)** — stunning red & white colonial architecture, most historical landmark
- **Lotus Tower** — South Asia's tallest tower, panoramic city views, great for skyline shots
- **Galle Face Green** — iconic promenade, spend the evening
- **Independence Memorial Hall**
- **Colombo National Museum**
- **Sri Kailasanathar Swami Devasthanam Temple**
- **Mount Lavinia Beach** — beautiful beach just outside Colombo
- **Pettah Market** — chaos, cheapest finds

### Day Trips from Colombo
- Galle (2 hrs) — Galle Fort & colonial charm
- Kandy (3.5 hrs) — Temple of the Sacred Tooth + botanical gardens
- Sigiriya (4 hrs) — Rock Fortress
- Bentota (1.5 hrs) — golden beaches + water sports
- Hikkaduwa (2 hrs) — coral reefs + snorkelling

### Cultural Triangle / North-Central
- **Sigiriya Rock Fortress (UNESCO)** — Lion's Paw entrance, ancient frescoes, summit views; climb at 5:30am
- **Polonnaruwa viewpoint** — best external view of Sigiriya Rock
- **Dambulla Cave Temple** — 150+ gilded Buddhas
- **Minneriya National Park** — elephant gathering (sometimes 100+)
- **Sigiriya Wildlife Safari** — wild elephants in herds

### Kandy
- **Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (UNESCO)** — most sacred Buddhist site in Sri Lanka
- **Royal Botanical Gardens, Peradeniya**
- **Kandy Lake** — evening walk
- Cultural Kandyan dance show

### Ella
- **Nine Arch Bridge** — colonial railway bridge through jungle, most Instagrammable spot (6:15–6:45am)
- **Upper Diyaluma Falls** — natural infinity pools, far better than Ravana Falls (~45 min, before 9am, free)
- **Little Adam's Peak** — sunrise hike from 98 Acres side (4:30–5am start)
- **Ella Famous Train Ride** — Ella → Dimodara, passes tea estates + tunnels + famous loop; stop at Demodara station
- **Halpewatte Tea Factory** — 3,000 LKR guided tour
- Ashok Vatika (near Kandy direction)

### Galle & South
- **Galle Fort (UNESCO)** — Dutch-era colonial buildings, lighthouse, ramparts, boutiques; best at sunset
- **Stilt fishermen, Koggala** — between Galle and Ahangama, best late afternoon
- **Dalawella Beach / rope swing** — Unawatuna side, famous swing right on the beach
- **Hiriketiya Bay** — small horseshoe-shaped cove, most-saved drone shot in Sri Lanka
- **Silent Beach** — untouched, near Dikwella
- **Secret Beach, Mirissa** — hard to reach, worth it; also Parrot Rock + Coconut Hill

### Wildlife & Nature
- **Mirissa whale watching** — blue + sperm whales + dolphins, Nov–Apr; Raja & The Whales recommended
- **Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery** — baby turtles, watch them released into sea
- **Bentota River Safari** — mangroves, monitor lizards, cinnamon island
- **Yala National Park** — safari, stay within the park
