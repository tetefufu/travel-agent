## NEW LOCATIONS FROM PDF (Not in current HTML)

### Negombo
- Airport arrival base
- Calm, unhurried beach town
- Sunset walks
- Close to airport for easy start

### Knuckles Mountain Range
- UNESCO Knuckles Conservation Forest
- Protected from mass tourism
- **Pekoe Trail access** — 300+ km long-distance trail (22 stages)
  - Sections through: hill towns, tea country, villages, forests, viewpoints
  - Can do partial stages, not full trail
- Guided hiking options
- Raw, untouched nature
- **Strategic alternative to Kandy town** — use Knuckles as hiking base instead of busy Kandy

### Haputale
- Tea-country headquarters
- **Lipton's Seat sunrise** — famous panoramic tea-estate viewpoint
- Tea-estate walks (guided short walks)
- Pekoe Trail sections available
- Quieter than Ella
- Slower-paced reset
- Cloud-draped landscapes

### Pekoe Trail (Long-Distance Walking Trail)
- Sri Lanka's official 300+ km long-distance walking route
- 22 stages total
- Sections through: hill towns, tea country, villages, forests, viewpoints
- Runs through Kandy region, Haputale, Ella area
- Can be done in stages (not all-or-nothing)
- Haputale noted as strong hiking base with Pekoe Trail access
- Authentic, slow-paced experience

### Arugam Bay — East Coast Surf Hub
- Surf lessons at:
  - **Whiskey Point**
  - **Peanut Farm**
- Laid-back beach town
- East coast in-season for August
- Optional Kumana lagoon day trip
- Relaxed vibe vs commercial southern beaches
- Best beach call for August

### Kumana National Park
- Day trip from Arugam Bay
- More wildlife if interested
- Alternative to extended Yala stay

### Pasikudah Beach
- Alternative to Trincomalee
- Shallow turquoise bay
- Calmer, more relaxed
- Good for swimming
- Optional boat/snorkel in good conditions

### Whale Watching — Trincomalee (August-Specific)
- Best time: through August
- Different species/seasonality than Mirissa
- Trinco whale watching is specifically noted for August viability
