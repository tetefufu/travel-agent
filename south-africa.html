Eid al-Adha 2026: Jun 6–7. Excellent timing — early June is perfect for both Cape Town (mild 18–22°C, no crowds) AND the start of dry safari season. Game viewing improves rapidly through June as bush thins. Strongly recommended for this window. Note: Cape Town is in the Southern Hemisphere — June is winter there, but very mild and pleasant.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
None — UK passport · 30 days visa-free · passport must be valid 30 days beyond stay
💵 Currency
South African Rand (ZAR) · 1 ZAR ≈ 0.2 AED · 100 ZAR ≈ 20 AED · Cards widely accepted
💸 Tipping
10–15% at restaurants · R20–50 for safari staff (very important)
🚗 Getting around
Cape Town: Uber recommended · rent a car for Winelands & Peninsula. Safari: all transfers included in lodge package
🌡️ June weather
Cape Town: 14–18°C (mild, occasional rain) · Kruger: 15–26°C (dry, perfect)
🦟 Health
No malaria prophylaxis needed for Cape Town · Consult doctor for Greater Kruger area
📷 Photography
June light in Kruger is stunning — carry 300mm+ lens for wildlife
🏠 Best base
Cape Town: Bantry Bay / Camps Bay area. Safari: private reserves near Kruger or KZN
