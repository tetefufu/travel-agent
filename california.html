Best time: September–October. Warm and dry in SF (20–26°C), Napa Valley harvest season (Sep–Oct is crush season — the best time to visit wineries), Big Sur sunny & clear. June works for the SF sightseeing and Big Sur coast, but Napa can be hot (35°C+). For Eid al-Adha (Jun 6–7), an alternative July or September trip is recommended for the best all-round experience. However June still delivers for the cultural and coastal highlights.

🍽️ Restaurants

🌿 California is outstanding for vegetarians. San Francisco has one of the world's best plant-based dining scenes. Indian, Mexican, Pan-Asian, and Californian cuisine all have exceptional vegetarian offerings. Bay Area is the birthplace of California cuisine — farm-to-table with abundant produce. Napa Valley farm-fresh vegetable menus are world-class.
