Carnival recommendation: If you want the full Trinidad experience, go in February for Carnival — this is one of the world's greatest street parties, rivalling Rio. Trinidad Carnival 2026 falls on Feb 16–17 (Mon–Tue) before Ash Wednesday. Book hotels 6+ months ahead — accommodation sells out fast and prices triple. Strongly recommended: time your trip to include both Carnival days. For Eid al-Adha (Jun 6–7), June is the start of the wet season with short afternoon showers — prices at their lowest, great birdwatching. Two excellent but very different trip windows.
