Eid al-Adha: Jun 6–7. Perfect timing. June = dry season begins, wildlife concentrating at waterholes, cool nights (10–15°C). Book fly-in camps 6+ months in advance — they fill very fast. Strongly recommend June.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
None · UK passport · visa-free up to 90 days
💵 Currency
Botswana Pula (BWP) · 1 BWP ≈ 0.27 AED · 1 USD ≈ 13.5 BWP
💸 Tipping
Camp staff: $10–20/day/couple · guides: $20–30/day · all in USD or BWP
🚁 Getting around
Charter flights between camps — all arranged by camps. No road between Okavango camps.
💉 Health
Malaria prophylaxis required — consult GP before travel. Yellow fever cert may be needed if transiting endemic areas.
🗣️ Language
English (official) · Setswana · universally English at camps
🌡️ June weather
Days 22–26°C · nights 5–12°C · bring warm layer for dawn game drives
🏠 Packing
Neutral colours (khaki/green/brown) · no white/black · binoculars · camera with zoom
