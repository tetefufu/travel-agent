🍽️ Vegetarian Indian Food — Detailed Guide

India is the world capital of vegetarian cuisine. Over 30% of the population is vegetarian — an extraordinary range awaits. Every region has distinct traditions. As Indian food lovers, this is your ultimate culinary destination.

In India, vegetarianism is the norm — most fine dining hotels offer complete vegetarian menus. Rajasthan is particularly renowned for its meat-free Marwari cuisine. Goa has exceptional Goan vegetarian (influenced by Portuguese, Hindu and Catholic traditions). Delhi's North Indian and Mughlai vegetarian dishes are world-class.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
e-Visa required for UK passports · Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in · ~$25 (≈ AED 92) · Tourist e-Visa (1 year, multiple entry) · Apply 4+ days before travel
💰 Currency
Indian Rupee (INR) · 1 INR ≈ 0.044 AED · ATMs in cities · Cards at luxury hotels · Carry cash for bazaars/auto-rickshaws
💸 Tipping
10% at restaurants · 100–200 INR (4–9 AED) for drivers/guides · Tip spa staff directly
🚗 Getting around
Delhi: Metro (fastest) + Uber · Rajasthan: hire private car + driver for entire Rajasthan leg (~AED 550/day) · Goa: scooter hire or hired driver
🗣️ Language
Hindi + English · English widely spoken at all luxury properties · No issues at Aman/Taj/RAAS level
⏰ Time zone
IST: UTC+5:30 · 1.5h ahead of UAE
🌡️ Weather (Oct–Mar)
Delhi: 10–26°C (cold nights Nov–Jan — pack layers) · Rajasthan: 14–28°C days, cold nights · Goa: 26–32°C perfect
🏥 Health
Drink only bottled water · Avoid raw salads from street vendors · Hepatitis A/Typhoid vaccines advisable · Excellent private hospitals (Fortis/Max) · Travel insurance essential
📱 SIM card
Airtel tourist SIM at airport · requires passport · ~AED 20 for 30 days unlimited data
