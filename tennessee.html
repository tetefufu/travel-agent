🍽️ Restaurants & Vegetarian Guide

⚠️ Vegetarian note: Nashville is traditionally a meat-heavy BBQ city, but the dining scene has evolved rapidly. Several farm-to-table and modern American restaurants offer excellent vegetarian menus. Hot chicken restaurants are almost entirely meat-based — skip those. The best vegetarian experiences are at upscale farm-to-table spots.
