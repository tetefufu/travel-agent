Best window: July–October for the Great Wildebeest Migration across the Mara River — one of the greatest wildlife spectacles on Earth. July is prime. Eid al-Adha (Jun 6–7) falls just before peak season — consider travelling Jun 4–13 to catch early migration arrivals while prices are slightly lower. Eid al-Fitr (Mar 30–31) falls in the rainy season — not recommended for Kenya safari.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
e-Visa required · ~$50 (≈183 AED) · Apply at evisa.go.ke · 3–5 days processing
💶 Currency
Kenyan Shilling (KES) · USD widely accepted at camps · 1 USD ≈ 3.67 AED
💸 Tipping
Safari guide: ~$20–30/day per couple (73–110 AED) · camp staff: $5–10/day each
🚗 Getting around
All transfers arranged by camps · internal charter flights essential (Mara)
💉 Health
Yellow fever cert required · Malaria prophylaxis recommended · consult GP
🗣️ Language
English (official) · Swahili · English universally spoken at luxury camps
📸 Photography
Bring telephoto lens (200–400mm) · dust protector for camera gear
👗 Dress code
Neutral colours (khaki/olive) for safari · no white or bright colours in bush
