🇵🇪 Option 1 — Peru: Cusco + Machu Picchu

⭐ Our Top Pick

🇵🇪 Peru — Cusco · Sacred Valley · Machu Picchu

Planning Stage

Machu Picchu is one of the world's Seven Wonders — a once-in-a-lifetime Inca citadel at 2,430m. Paired with Cusco's colonial luxury and the Sacred Valley, this is transformative travel. Belmond runs the finest hotels in the region and the legendary Hiram Bingham train.

Eid al-Adha Jun 6–7 2026 — PERFECT. June is dry season in Peru, clear skies, comfortable temperatures at altitude. Book Machu Picchu entry permits well in advance — they sell out months ahead.

🇨🇴 Option 2 — Colombia: Cartagena + Medellín

Option 2

🇨🇴 Colombia — Cartagena · Medellín

Planning Stage

Cartagena is one of the Caribbean's most beautiful colonial cities — candy-coloured walls, rooftop pools, crystal-clear sea. Lower cost than Peru or Brazil. Medellín ("City of Eternal Spring") has transformed dramatically and has a thriving luxury scene. Together they make an ideal 12-day pairing.

🇧🇷 Option 3 — Brazil: Rio de Janeiro

Option 3

🇧🇷 Brazil — Rio de Janeiro + Optional Iguazú Falls

Planning Stage

Rio is one of the world's most jaw-dropping cities — Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana, Sugarloaf Mountain, and the legendary Belmond Copacabana Palace. More complex logistically and requires more safety awareness, but the payoff is immense. Add Iguazú Falls (Argentina/Brazil border) for a world wonder detour.

Eid al-Adha Jun 6–7 2026 — GOOD. June is dry season in Rio — cooler (20–24°C), lower humidity, no Carnival madness. Good availability at Belmond Copacabana Palace. Note: Brazil visa for UK passports is currently visa-free (check latest rules — this changed in 2023). Confirm before booking.
