## Initial Research Findings

### Best Time to Go
- **Dry season (May–October):** Best weather, higher prices, more crowds
- **Shoulder (April, November):** Good weather + lower prices + fewer crowds
- **Wet season (Nov–Apr):** Warm, occasional rain, cheapest but less predictable
- **Eid alignment:** Need to check 2026 Eid dates + your preferred travel window

### Flights (Dubai → Bora Bora)
- **Economy:** ~$1,597–$1,598 USD (~5,900 AED) *one-way*
- **Business class:** Requires direct quote; typically 2–3x economy
- **Airlines:** Emirates, Air Tahiti Nui, Air Tahiti
- **Tip:** Book 2–4 months ahead; Sunday bookings ~6–13% cheaper

### Hotels (Overwater Bungalows)
| Hotel | Type | Price/Night | Notes |
|-------|------|-------------|-------|
| St. Regis Bora Bora | Overwater Deluxe | $2,200–$2,500 | Island/Otemanu views |
| St. Regis Bora Bora | Royal Otemanu Suite (1BR) | $4,500–$5,500 | Premium positioning |
| Four Seasons | Overwater | $2,000–$3,000 (off) / $2,500–$8,000 (peak) | Variable by season |

### Multi-Island Options (Inter-Island Flights)
- **Rangiroa:** $636 RT from Bora Bora (snorkeling, relaxed vibes)
- **Fakarava:** $770 RT from Bora Bora (remote, diving, pristine)
- **Air Tahiti Multi-Pass:** Available for island-hopping (check flexibility)

### Vegetarian Dining
- **Fine dining:** Saint James (waterfront), Le Corail (French), La Villa Mahana
- **Casual:** Bamboo (sushi with vegan options), resort dining (can customize)
- **Note:** Smaller island dining (Fakarava/Rangiroa) likely more limited
