Restaurants & Dining

📌 Flexitarian Note: Your travel companion eats sushi, cooked prawns, small amounts of chicken—NO beef. Most restaurants can accommodate. Starred options below highlight best fit.

Casual Local

Snack Matira (Matira Beach)

Menu: Fish burger (pan-fried tuna), poisson cru, pizzas, steak, sandwiches, omelets, salads, ice cream

✅ Excellent

  • Why: Fish burger is standout (fresh tuna), poisson cru, salads abundant. Flexitarian-friendly.
  • Vibe: Open-air beachside, walk-in only (first come, first served), palm-shaded tables
  • Hours: 10am–4pm daily

Aloe Cafe (Vaitape)

Menu: Poisson cru, crepes, paninis, salads, kabobs, pizza, pastries, homemade ice cream

✅ Excellent

  • Why: Fresh pastries, ice cream, flexible menu. Poisson cru and salads work perfectly.
  • Hours: Mon–Sat 6:30am–6pm (breakfast 6:30–10:30am, lunch 11am–4pm)
  • Known for: Homemade pastries, Belgian beers, tropical cocktails

Fine Dining

Bloody Mary's (Iconic waterfront)

Menu: Fresh daily seafood, vegetarian options (grilled cheese with tofu), salads, beef, chicken

✅ Excellent

  • Why: Famous for fresh daily seafood, explicitly offers vegetarian/flexitarian options
  • Vibe: Iconic, Instagram-worthy, casual yet famous

La Villa Mahana (Most romantic spot on island)

Menu: Seared ahi tuna with vanilla, seafood salad & caviar, foie gras, mahi mahi curry & banana, filet de bœuf, risotto de langouste, chocolate fondant, banane flambée

⚠️ Limited

  • Good for: Mahi mahi curry, risotto de langouste (lobster), tuna preparations
  • Avoid: Filet de bœuf (beef)
  • Tasting menu: 5-course Chef's Tasting available
  • Pricing: ~120–180 AED per person with wine

Le Saint James (Lagoon-side, Vaitape)

Menu: Polynesian Plate (various tuna preparations), foie gras, duck, lamb, pork belly, pan-fried scallops in coconut crust

✅ Good

  • Best for: Tuna preparations (raw & seared), pan-fried scallops
  • Hours: Breakfast 6:30–9:30am, lunch 11am–2pm, dinner 6–9pm (closed Sundays)
  • Pricing: Starters ~30 AED; dinner ~120–180 AED with wine

Resort Fine Dining

St. Regis — Lagoon by Jean-Georges (Overwater, signature)

Menu: Yellowfin tuna sashimi (soy-ginger), black pepper-crusted mahi-mahi, Tahitian vanilla features, fresh Pacific fish

✅ Excellent

  • Why: Sashimi, fresh fish, Asian spice — perfect for flexitarian
  • Signature: Jean-Georges Vongerichten's French-Asian fusion

Four Seasons — Vaimiti Asian (Overwater)

Menu: Sushi, sashimi, red tuna sashimi, pad thai, wagyu beef, sake collection. Separate vegan menu.

✅ Excellent

  • Why: Sushi, sashimi, pad thai perfect. Dedicated vegan menu with tofu, vegetable stir-fry.
  • Cuisines: Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indian

Four Seasons — Arii Moana (Mediterranean)

Menu: Red tuna crudo with watermelon & yuzu, lobster with roasted clementine, burrata, risotto with lobster medallions, grilled beef fillet

✅ Good

  • Best for: Tuna crudo, lobster risotto, burrata + heirloom tomatoes
  • Limited: Menu beef-heavy; recommend focusing on seafood options

Westin — 'O'A Bar (Cocktails & appetizers)

Menu: Cocktails (Otemanu Sunset with pineapple, Coconut Mojito, Tahitian Negroni), bar bites, rum-tasting

✅ Good

  • Vibe: Sunset drinks, tropical cocktails, casual
  • Note: Better for aperitif than full meal
  • Tip: Arrive early for sunset seating

InterContinental — Le Corail (Fine Dining, French)

Menu: 5-course or 7-course French tasting menus. Local lagoon fish (Panaha), seasonal rotation. Largest wine cellar in French Polynesia.

⚠️ Limited

  • Good for: Local lagoon fish (Panaha), can request specific accommodations
  • French cuisine: Meat-heavy; request fish-focused tasting with advance notice
  • Hours: 6:30–9:30pm (closed Mon & Fri)
  • Wine: Largest cellar in French Polynesia

Activities

📌 Swimming Note: You don't swim in the sea, but we've curated a mix of land activities + a few signature shallow-water experiences (tours, lagoon views, manta rays in shallow water).

Must-Do Shallow-Water Experiences

🦑 Manta Ray Encounter (Shallow Lagoon)

Spot manta rays in clear shallow lagoon water — no swimming required, boat-based tour. Peak season (May) is best.

🚤 Shared Lagoon Tour

Boat tour exploring multiple snorkeling spots. Watch others snorkel or enjoy lagoon views from boat. Often includes fish feeding.

Land-Based Activities

🏨 Overwater Bungalow Experience

Stay in iconic overwater bungalow with lagoon views, direct access. Peak experience — wake to crystal-clear water, glass-bottom floor.

🏔️ Mount Otemanu Hiking

Extinct volcano trek. Scenic, challenging. Best in dry season (May–October).

🥾 Valley of the Kings Trek (Anua)

Scenic valley walk, lush scenery, cultural sites. Moderate difficulty.

🛵 ATV Tour on Main Island

Guided off-road adventure exploring island interior, villages, scenic viewpoints.

👨‍🍳 Cultural Experience & Cooking Class

Learn Polynesian cooking techniques, local ingredients, traditional methods. Often includes meal.

🛍️ Explore Vaitape & Local Markets

Main town: shopping, local crafts, pearl vendors, French Polynesian goods.

🌅 Sunset Cruise

Evening boat tour with tropical cocktails, island views, relaxed vibe.

🏖️ Matira Beach Exploration

Iconic white-sand beach. Walk, lounge, enjoy beach restaurants (Snack Matira nearby).

Seating Tips for Panoramic Views

  • LAX → Bora Bora (arriving): Sit on left side for stunning island views
  • Bora Bora → Papeete (departing): Sit on right side for views of the island
  • Papeete → onward: Sit on right side to see Moorea and sister islands

Practical Information

  • Visa: UK passport holders get visa-free entry for up to 90 days
  • Currency: Prices shown in AED (equivalent). Local currency is CFP franc (XPF)
  • Getting Around: Rental car, taxi, or hotel shuttle recommended
  • Tipping: Not required but appreciated (5–10% for good service)
  • Best Months: May–October (dry season, best weather)
