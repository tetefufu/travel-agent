# Bora Bora — Organized Research

## Notes & Timing

- **Best season:** May to October (high season, best for water activities/boat tours)
- **Budget season:** End of April (more rain but better hotel deals)
- **Island experience:** 4 days is ideal to experience Bora Bora
- **Mountain:** Mount Otemanu (extinct volcano) — hiking available

## Getting There

### Flight Routes
- **Paris Orly → Papeete (PPT) → Bora Bora:** Air Tahiti (short domestic flight)
- **Paris → Papeete via LA:** Common alternative
- **London → Papeete via San Francisco:** Common alternative
- **Dubai to Papeete:** Air Moana or Air Tahiti Nui

### Seating Tips
- **Bora Bora flights (going):** Sit on left side for panoramic island views
- **Bora Bora flights (departing):** Sit on right side for views
- **Return to Papeete:** Sit on right side to see Moorea and other local islands

## Restaurants

### Local / Casual

**Snack Matira** (Matira Beach)
- Menu: Fish burger, poisson cru, pizzas, steak frites, sandwiches, omelets, salads, ice cream
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ✅ Good — fish burger (pan-fried tuna), poisson cru, salads, vegetarian options available on request
- Vibe: Open-air, beachside, palm-shaded, walk-in only (first come, first served)
- Hours: 10am–4pm daily (lunch only)

**Aloe Cafe** (Vaitape)
- Menu: Poisson cru, crepes, paninis, salads, kabobs, pizza, hamburgers, homemade pastries, ice cream
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ✅ Good — poisson cru, salads, paninis, pastries, ice cream
- Hours: Mon–Sat 6:30am–6pm (breakfast 6:30–10:30am, lunch 11am–4pm)
- Known for: Fresh pastries, homemade ice cream, Belgian beers, tropical cocktails

**Chez Ben's**
- Menu: Limited info available — appears to be casual local spot

### Fine Dining / Instagram-worthy

**Bloody Mary's** (Popular, iconic)
- Menu: Fresh daily seafood, vegetarian grilled cheese, tofu veggie burger, salads, beef and chicken options
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ✅ Excellent — explicitly offers vegetarian options, fresh seafood daily
- Vibe: Famous waterfront dining
- Note: Casual yet famous — good for Instagram

**La Villa Mahana** (Most romantic spot on island)
- Menu: French-Polynesian fine dining. Starters: seared ahi tuna with vanilla, seafood salad with caviar, foie gras. Mains: Mahi mahi curry & banana, filet de bœuf à la vanille (beef), risotto de langouste (lobster). Desserts: Chocolate fondant, banane flambée
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ⚠️ Limited — beef-heavy (filet de bœuf), but mahi mahi and risotto de langouste work well; no explicit vegetarian options mentioned
- Offers: Chef's tasting menu (5 courses), à la carte
- Pricing: ~10,000–15,000 XPF (~120–180 AED) per person with wine

**Le Saint James** (Vaitape, lagoon side)
- Menu: French-Polynesian fusion. Polynesian Plate (various tuna preparations, raw & seared), foie gras with chutney, duck breast, lamb, crispy pork belly, pan-fried scallops in coconut crust
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ✅ Good — raw tuna, pan-fried scallops, though mostly meat/fish focused
- Hours: Breakfast 6:30–9:30am, lunch 11am–2pm, dinner 6–9pm (closed Sundays)
- Pricing: Starters ~2,500–3,500 XPF; dinner ~10,000–15,000 XPF with wine

### Resort Restaurants (Luxury)

**St. Regis Bora Bora — Lagoon by Jean-Georges** (Overwater)
- Menu: French-Asian fusion by Jean-Georges Vongerichten. Yellowfin tuna sashimi (soy-ginger), black pepper-crusted mahi-mahi, fresh local fish, Pacific yellowfin, Tahitian vanilla features
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ✅ Excellent — sashimi, fresh fish, Asian-inspired preparations
- Features: Locally sourced ingredients, Pacific seafood, Tahitian vanilla
- Hours: Dinner service

**Four Seasons — Vaimiti Asian** (Overwater)
- Menu: Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indian. Sushi, sashimi, red tuna sashimi, pad thai, wagyu beef, sake
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ✅ Excellent — vegan menu available (tofu ponzu, coconut soup, vegetable stir-fry, pineapple tofu fried rice). Sushi, sashimi, pad thai all work
- Hours: Dinner 5–9:30pm (bar until 10:30pm)
- Note: Separate vegan menu; extensive sake collection

**Four Seasons — Arii Moana** (Mediterranean)
- Menu: Mediterranean classics. Red tuna crudo with watermelon & yuzu, lobster with roasted clementine, burrata, risotto with lobster medallions, grilled beef fillet
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ⚠️ Limited — beef-focused menu, though lobster and crudo tuna work; burrata + heirloom tomatoes option
- Desserts: Valrhona chocolate lava cake, tiramisu

**Westin — 'O'A Bar** (Cocktails & Bar Bites)
- Menu: International fare, cocktails (Otemanu Sunset, Coconut Mojito, Tahitian Negroni), sunset views
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: Casual bar setting — good for drinks/appetizers, not full meal
- Features: Rum-tasting classes, curated wine list
- Note: Arrive early for sunset seating

**InterContinental — Le Corail** (Fine Dining, French)
- Menu: Gourmet French with Polynesian touches. 5-course or 7-course tasting menus. Largest wine cellar in French Polynesia
- Vegetarian/flexitarian: ⚠️ Limited info — French cuisine typically meat-heavy, but local lagoon fish (Panaha) featured. Can accommodate allergies/dietary restrictions with advance notice
- Hours: 6:30–9:30pm (closed Mon & Fri)
- Seasonal 7-course tasting menu rotates
