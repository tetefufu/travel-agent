mount otemanu - extinct volcano
high season - may to october; best time for snorkeling, diving, boat tours
end of april - more chance of rain but better hotel deals
travel option to check out - Paris Orly to Papeete (PPT); Then short domestic flight Air Tahiti to Bora Bora; Air France and Air Tahiti Nui also offer connections.
Other common routes - Paris to Papeete via LA; London to Papeete via San Fran
How to get to Papeete - Air Moana or Air Tahiti Nui
When flying to Bora Bora sit on the left side for panoramic views; When departing Bora Bora sit on the right side
On the way back to Papeete sit on the right side for views of other local islands - Moorea 
Hotels - FS Bora Bora, St Regis Bora Bora, Westin Bora Bora
Book shared excursions - opt for a shared lagoon tour
Island hoping - 4 days is the ideal amount of time to experience Bora Bora
Things to do - stay in an overwater bubgalow, snorkeling, scuba diving, swin with manta rays and sharks, ATV tour on main island, sunset cruise, Matira beach, paddle boarding, cultural experience and cooking classes, hiking mount otemanu, trek valley of the kings (anua) - scenic trail, shopping, explore Vaitape, shared boat tour to explore multiple snorkelling spots.
Where to eat - Bloody Mary's, Snack Matira, Le Saint James, Chez Ben's, Aloe Cafe, La Villa Mahana (most romantic spots on the island)
Best Resort Restaurants - Lagoon by Jean-Georges (St Regis), Vaimiti Asian (FS), Ari Moana (FS), 'O'A Bar (Westin), Le Corail (Inter Continenal)
