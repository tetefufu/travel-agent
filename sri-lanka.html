Best time: December–March for Galle + Colombo (west coast). June = monsoon on west coast — Galle & Colombo get heavy rains. However, Sigiriya (north-central) remains dry in June. If travelling over Eid (Jun 6–7), consider a north-focused itinerary: Sigiriya + Cultural Triangle + possible east coast (Trincomalee is dry Jun–Sep). Strongly recommend Dec–Mar for the full itinerary below.

🍽️ Restaurants

🌿 Sri Lanka is outstanding for vegetarians. Traditional Sri Lankan cuisine is naturally plant-forward: rice & curry, dhal, jackfruit curry, mallum (greens), pol sambol (coconut), hoppers, string hoppers, kottu. Almost every local restaurant has 80%+ vegetarian dishes.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
ETA required · UK passport · ~$35 (≈129 AED) · apply at eta.gov.lk · 30 days extendable
💵 Currency
Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) · 1 USD ≈ 300–320 LKR · 1 AED ≈ 82–88 LKR
💸 Tipping
10% in restaurants · drivers 500–1,000 LKR/day · guides 1,000–2,000 LKR/day
🚗 Getting around
Private driver + car essential — ~550–735 AED/day all-in. Tuk-tuks for short trips (~55–110 AED).
🌡️ Dec–Mar weather
28–32°C · low humidity · west coast clear · ideal beach conditions
🗣️ Language
Sinhala / Tamil · English widely spoken · no issues for tourists
💉 Health
Hepatitis A recommended. No malaria in south/west coastal areas. Water: bottled only.
🏠 Dress code
Modest at temples (cover shoulders/knees). Beaches: standard swimwear fine.
