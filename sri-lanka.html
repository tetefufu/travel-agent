Best time: December–March. West coast (Galle, Weligama, Mirissa) dry and clear. Whale watching peaks Dec–Apr. Eid al-Fitr 2026: Mar 30–31 — perfect alignment. Strongly recommend late March. June = monsoon on west coast — north-central (Sigiriya) stays dry but south coast gets heavy rain. Avoid September (budget period).

🗺️ South Coast — Area Guide

South Sri Lanka is not just Mirissa. Each area has a distinct vibe — stay where it matches yours. Everything is 20–40 min apart.

Hiriketiya

🏄 Best for us

  • Small aesthetic bay
  • Stylish boutique cafés
  • Good surf
  • Nice sunset energy
  • Curated, low-key crowd
Ahangama

☕ Café capital

  • Trendy café scene
  • Photo spots
  • Surf breaks
  • Calm but cool vibe
  • Best sunset cocktails
Weligama

🌊 Relaxed surf town

  • Beginner-friendly surf
  • Café hopping
  • Digital nomad vibe
  • More chill than Mirissa
Mirissa

🐋 Whale watching base

  • Best nightlife on coast
  • Beach bars, party energy
  • Secret Beach worth it
  • Parrot Rock · Coconut Hill
Unawatuna

🌴 Family-friendly

  • Calm beaches
  • Close to Galle (15 min)
  • Dalawella rope swing
  • Turtle sightings
Galle Fort

🏰 Colonial history

  • UNESCO fort walls
  • Boutique shopping
  • Sunset walks
  • Half-day side trip
District clarity: Galle District = Galle, Unawatuna, Ahangama. Matara District = Weligama, Mirissa, Madiha, Dikwella, Hiriketiya. Don't backtrack — plan by zone.

ℹ️ Practical Info

🛂 Visa
ETA required · UK passport · ~$35 (≈129 AED) · apply at eta.gov.lk · 30 days, extendable
💵 Currency
Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) · 1 AED ≈ 82–88 LKR · carry cash for smaller towns
💸 Tipping
10% restaurants · drivers 500–1,000 LKR/day · guides 1,000–2,000 LKR/day
🚗 Transport
Private driver essential — 550–735 AED/day all-in. Tuk-tuks for short distances (55–110 AED). No need to drive yourself.
🌡️ Dec–Mar
28–32°C · low humidity · west coast dry · ideal beach + sightseeing
🚂 Trains
Kandy→Ella: book weeks ahead via 12Go Asia. Sit on the right side. Ella→Badulla: quieter, same landscapes, nearly empty carriages.
💉 Health
Hepatitis A recommended. No malaria in south/west. Bottled water only.
🏠 Temples
Cover shoulders + knees. Beaches: standard swimwear. No issues.
🛒 What to buy
Ceylon tea · cinnamon · batik clothing · handmade souvenirs · Ayurvedic products
📍 Navigation
Google Maps works well. Tuk-tuk drivers know everything locally — use them for short hops.
Insider tips from saved intel: Don't backtrack daily — plan your route as one continuous loop south (Ahungalla → Sigiriya → Kandy → Ella → Galle coast → Weligama → Colombo). Tuk-tuks work best for short distances. Plan sunset spots in advance — evening traffic on south coast is real.
